Stop 2 · Bibliotheksausweis

Your CampusCard is your library card

No separate registration. The card opens the doors at any hour and lets you borrow. The library number printed on it is your catalogue username.

Report it at once, so nobody borrows on your account
Moved
Tell the library: late reminders 3 and 4 come by registered post
At night
The doors only open with your card

Ausleihe · Verlängerung · Rückgabe

Borrow, renew or return a book

Borrow

  1. Find it in the catalogue and note its Signatur
  2. Take it from the shelf
  3. Use the self-check terminal with your CampusCard, or the desk in staffed hours
  4. Watch your university email for the due-date reminder

Return

  1. Self-check terminal inside, or
  2. The return boxes outside the library, at any hour
  3. Check your account in the catalogue to confirm it is gone

Renew

  1. Students: loans renew automatically, 4 weeks at a time, up to 6 times
  2. Renewal stops if someone places a hold (Vormerkung). Then return by the due date
  3. Check current due dates in your account in the catalogue
When is it due?

Estimate only. Exam periods are shorter; your catalogue account shows the real date.
